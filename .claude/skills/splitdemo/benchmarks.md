# Benchmarks for the calculator

Numbers to fill in a rate the prospect did not state. Read the row for the chain you are building, take the low end, and put the source in the input's `note` (80 characters). Where the table says no source, write your own low guess, leave `note` out, and list it as a guess in your report.

Gathered November 2026 from the pages linked. Vendor reports count their own customers; every study of reply speed shows what goes together, not what causes what. That is why these fill estimates the owner can change, and never a claim in a caption.

## Cold email

| Number | Value | Source and how far to trust it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Replies per email sent | 3.4% on average, 5.5% for the best quarter of senders | [Instantly, 2025 data](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026). A sending tool's own customers. |
| Replies per email sent, large B2B sample | 0.45% | [Belkins, 7.5M emails](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates). Use it as the floor. |
| Emails per meeting | 344, about 0.3% | [Gong, 28M emails](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/does-cold-email-even-work-any-more-heres-what-the-data-says/). Sales teams at larger companies. |
| Positive replies and meetings, per prospect | about 2.2% and 1.2% | [SmartReach, first half of 2026](https://smartreach.io/reports/state-of-cold-email/). Counted per prospect, so it reads higher than a per-email rate. |
| Open rate | no source | Apple Mail registers opens that never happened. Leave "opened" out of the chain and the funnel. |
| Reply to meeting, meeting to client | no source | Guess low: half of replies lead to a talk, one talk in five becomes a client. |

## Speed of the first reply

| Number | Value | Source and how far to trust it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How fast firms answer a web lead | 42 hours on average; 23% never answer | [HBR 2011, 2,241 firms](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads). Old, and still the largest audit. |
| What answering within the hour is worth | about 7 times as likely to qualify the lead | Same study. An association, so it supports a modest `add`, not a multiplier. |
| "100 times the odds at 5 minutes", "391% more conversions" | do not use | A 2007 study of six companies and a vendor figure whose original is gone. |
| Points of booking rate a fast reply adds | no source | Guess low: 10 to 20 points on a rate the owner states. |
