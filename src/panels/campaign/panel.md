# campaign

An email campaign: the sequence that goes out, and the counters that show how it is doing.

Pick it when: the system sends outreach, follow-ups or a newsletter in steps, and the proof is in opens, replies and bookings.

- Name the campaign after its audience: "Office managers in Denver".
- Title each step by what it says, with its day in `when`: "A first line about their office", "Day 1".
- `send` the steps in their order. A `note` says how many went: "40 sent".
- Start the counters at 0 and `stat` them as the story moves, ending on the one the owner is paid by: replies, visits, bookings.
- `status` takes the campaign from "Draft" to "Sending" to "Live".
