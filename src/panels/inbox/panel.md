# inbox

A mailbox: the list of mail, and next to it the opened mail with its reply.

Pick it when: the system reads, sorts, answers or sends email, or the customer gets a confirmation by mail.

- Say whose mailbox it is in the label: "Your inbox", "What the customer gets".
- Start with one to three ordinary mails, so the one that matters lands in a real inbox.
- `receive` the mail, `tag` it with what the system understood ("Interested", "Order status"), then `reply`. A `reply` opens the mail, so the viewer reads question and answer together.
- `label` on a reply says who wrote it. "Drafted for you" keeps the owner in charge of what goes out, and fits a demo whose outcome is a draft: "1 min to a ready reply". An outcome that counts a reply the customer received ("1 min to first reply") needs a reply that went out: "Sent for you".
- A body or a reply is the one or two sentences that prove the system understood.
- On a four-panel wall the list fits four mails, the ones that arrive included.
- Once a mail is open the list is narrow. A subject of about 20 characters fits there next to a tag, and `npm run shots` fails on one that ends in three dots.
