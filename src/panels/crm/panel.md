# crm

A pipeline board: one card per lead or deal, moving left to right through the stages.

Pick it when: the result is a lead, deal or job that reaches a stage: captured, qualified, booked, won.

- Use the prospect's own stage names, three or four of them, ending on the one that means money.
- Start with a card or two already on the board, so it looks like a pipeline in use.
- `add` the story's lead when the system captures it, `move` it when something real happens, and `set` a `tag` that says what: "Thu 10:00", "Quote sent".
- Put the amount in `value`. A card that reaches the last column with a price on it is the outcome made visible.
- A column fits four cards.
